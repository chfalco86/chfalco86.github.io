Chiara Falco

Economist · Independent Researcher

I am a development and environmental economist. I study how climate shocks, large infrastructure, trade policy and cultural norms shape rural livelihoods, land use and demographic behaviour, mainly in Brazil and Sub-Saharan Africa.

I work with microeconometrics, spatial data and data collected in the field. Alongside research, I work on impact evaluation and I am on the FAO Office of Evaluation roster of evaluation specialists.

Until 2026 I was Assistant Professor at the University of Milan, Department of Environmental Science and Policy. I hold the Italian National Scientific Qualification for Associate Professor (13/A2).
